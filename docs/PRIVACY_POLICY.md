# SwipeHire Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-11_

SwipeHire ("we", "us") operates the SwipeHire mobile app and website (the
"Service"), a recruitment platform connecting job seekers and employers in
Mongolia. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.
**This is a template — have it reviewed by a qualified adviser and replace the
contact details before publishing.**

## Who we are
- Service: SwipeHire
- Contact: tuguldurb0102@gmail.com

## Information we collect
Only what the Service needs to work:
- **Account**: email address, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), and
  your role (job seeker or employer).
- **Job-seeker profile**: name, age, contact details, location, skills,
  experience, education, salary expectation, profile photo, CV, and video CV
  that you choose to add.
- **Employer profile**: company name, registration number, contact, industry,
  and job postings you create.
- **Activity**: applications, saved items, and messages you send through the
  Service.
- **Technical**: basic device/log data needed to operate and secure the Service.

We do **not** knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; sign-up requires
confirming you are 18 or older.

## How we use it
- To create and operate your account and profile.
- To match job seekers with employers and enable applications and messaging.
- To secure the Service and prevent abuse.
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

## Storage and security
Data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and protected by
row-level security so users can only access their own data and data shared with
them. Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Private documents (CV, video, ID)
are kept in access-controlled storage and shared only through short-lived signed
links with authorised parties.

## Sharing
- **Employers** can see the profiles of job seekers who publish them, and the
  applications submitted to their jobs.
- **Job seekers** can see published job postings and the employers they interact
  with.
- Service providers (e.g. hosting) that process data on our behalf under
  contract. We do not otherwise share your data.

## Your rights and choices
- View and edit your profile in the app at any time.
- Unpublish your profile to stop appearing to employers.
- Request deletion of your account and associated data (in-app account deletion,
  or by contacting us). Some records may be retained where required by law.

## Data retention
We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the
Service, then delete or anonymise it, subject to legal retention requirements.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change and material
updates will be communicated in the app.
